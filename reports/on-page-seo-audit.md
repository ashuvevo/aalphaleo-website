## Strengths

- Titles, H1s, URLs, breadcrumbs, CTAs, related links, and canonical URLs are template-controlled.
- Blog pages include article metadata, related articles, FAQ content where provided, and JSON-LD.
- Location pages contain service/city H1s, local introduction, benefits, FAQ, related services, nearby cities, blog links, and CTA.
- The duplicate title between the blog and service speed-optimization pages was resolved.

## Remaining content-quality risks

- The 4,000 location pages are programmatically generated. Although the final crawl found no exact duplicate rendered text, Google evaluates *substantial similarity* and usefulness rather than exact duplicates. Prioritize genuine regional proof, local case studies, city-specific testimonials, and verified local details for the most commercially important city/service combinations.
- No explicit author bio, editorial policy, or reviewer information is present on all blog pages. Add author entity pages and editorial governance to strengthen experience and trust signals.
- The site uses logo-based Open Graph imagery across page types. Create distinct social images for priority services, case studies, and articles.
- Footer social links remain `#` placeholders until verified profile URLs are supplied.

## Keyword cannibalization controls

The site distinguishes service pages from city-service pages through URL, title, H1, canonical, and internal-link structure. Review Search Console query/page overlap quarterly, especially for broad phrases such as “Shopify development” and “SEO services”.
