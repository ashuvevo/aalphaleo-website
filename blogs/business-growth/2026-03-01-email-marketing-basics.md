## Best Practices

- Prioritize mobile experience—over 70% of Indian eCommerce traffic is mobile.
- Use original images and alt text optimized for accessibility and SEO.
- Keep navigation simple with clear paths to products, collections, and contact.
- Implement structured data (Product, FAQ, Organization) where relevant.
- Maintain consistent brand colors (#FE9B21, #FC6025, #F12A33) and typography across pages.
- Set up conversion tracking before scaling paid campaigns.
- Publish fresh content monthly to support email marketing basics authority.
- Secure your site with SSL, regular updates, and strong admin passwords.

These best practices reflect what we implement daily at Aalphaleo Digital Solution for Shopify and custom website clients. Consistency beats one-off optimizations.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Choosing cheap templates without considering speed, SEO, or conversion design.
- Ignoring Core Web Vitals and losing rankings due to slow Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).
- Duplicating manufacturer product descriptions—hurting SEO and differentiation.
- Running ads without proper tracking, leading to unmeasurable ROI.
- Overloading pages with apps and scripts that degrade performance.
- Neglecting email marketing basics after launch instead of treating it as ongoing optimization.
- Skipping user testing on real mobile devices and payment flows.

Most failed projects we audit share these patterns. Avoiding them saves months of rework and lost revenue.

If you recognize multiple items on this list, prioritize fixes by revenue impact before cosmetic changes.
